# Matched handoff diagnostic: search ordering

The [predeclared checkpoint protocol](matched_protocol.md) compared the frozen minimum-path-cost search with the conventional 2D map-visibility selector on three opened development scenarios. For each pair, an evaluator-only reset placed Gazebo at the **same physical handoff pose** from the earlier acquisition. Both policies received the same byte-identical acquired map, saved AMCL seed/uncertainty and sensor/commissioning files. The simulation clock was rebased to zero, retaining 420 s/18 m inspection, 36 captures and the original acquisition-distance contribution to the 35 m whole-task budget. No ground-truth pose was given to the robot. These are six **inspection-only diagnostic restarts**, not six uninterrupted missions or independent environments. [Freeze and checkpoint audit](../../results/matched_visibility_20261027/manifest_v3.json) · [Evaluator analysis](../../results/matched_visibility_20261027/analysis.json).

Two startup attempts for wrong/nearest are preserved: the first lacked the ROS setup and the second used a SLAM-specific `/pose` readiness gate despite this AMCL-only restart. Neither started physics. The third attempt used the appropriately scoped inspection readiness gate; the launcher-only revisions are retained as [v1](../../results/matched_visibility_20261027/manifest.json), [v2](../../results/matched_visibility_20261027/manifest_v2.json), and [v3](../../results/matched_visibility_20261027/manifest_v3.json). Eight attempts produced six scored missions. No algorithm, map, checkpoint, budget or sensor input changed across those revisions. [All attempts](../../results/matched_visibility_20261027/attempts.jsonl).

| Opened case | Nearest search | Visibility search |
|---|---|---|
| ID8 old, ID7 east | **No inspection**; first ID7 at 243.2 s / 15.05 m inspection travel; 32 captures; 18 m approach budget exhausted | **Correct complete inspection**; first ID7 at 143.1 s / 11.85 m; 17 captures |
| Moved ID7 east | Correct complete inspection; first ID7 at 213.7 s / 10.79 m; 33 captures | Correct complete inspection; first ID7 at 128.4 s / 9.25 m; 17 captures |
| ID7 absent, ID8 old | Honest `search_budget_exhausted`; 32 captures; 13.55 m / 250.8 s | Honest `search_budget_exhausted`; 32 captures; **17.50 m / 306.9 s** |

All six had zero contact, wrong-asset acceptance, and false completion; all three successful final claimed captures passed the independent physical judge. Restart physical position differences were below numerical precision within each pair; the first AMCL-ready position errors matched to displayed precision (wrong 0.140 m, moved 0.061 m, absent 0.040 m). Simulation scheduling, scan processing, AMCL particle evolution and controller stopping still vary slightly; this is a controlled single-pair comparison, not deterministic replay or a reliability estimate.

**Mechanism.** The score predicts the 360° area of free **2D occupancy-map cells** with unobstructed map-plane rays within 0.6–5 m, discounted by planned travel. It does not predict a 60° camera image, asset-facing direction, elevated occlusion, marker pixels or readability. At the old view on the wrong-asset map it favored east (17.52 m² newly predicted area, 4.64 m path, score 3.11) over center (12.31 m², 3.34 m, score 2.83). Both arms actually decoded ID7 at the **same eastern station** on their first suitable heading; their physical views were very similar: range 3.319 versus 3.330 m, gauge face about 41 px, and no readable gauge image. Thus the gain was **earlier useful camera coverage**, not a better per-image viewing angle or a shorter path to east. [Nearest camera trace](../../results/matched_visibility_20261027/mission_wrong_asset_nearest_infra_retry02/inspection/private/sensor_truth.jsonl) · [Gain camera trace](../../results/matched_visibility_20261027/mission_wrong_asset_gain/inspection/private/sensor_truth.jsonl) · [Policy decisions](../../results/matched_visibility_20261027/mission_wrong_asset_gain/inspection/method.jsonl).

At the first ID7 detection, nearest had about 2.95 m of the 18 m travel allowance left, while gain had 6.15 m; these are independently measured **physical-path proxies**, not odometry-budget telemetry. The map-planned approaches were 3.161 and 3.139 m. Nearest could not finish the approach; gain traveled 3.134 m after confirmation and made a physically valid final reading. The 100.2 s and 3.20 m earlier detection explain the result under this fixed budget. The absent case exposes a cost: gain ordering traveled about 3.95 m farther and took 56 s longer despite the same honest non-completion. Search efficiency, approach budget, and claims are therefore reported separately.

The pair isolates a useful conventional ordering effect on this acquired map and case. It does not establish a research contribution: established next-best-view methods already use visibility or information gain, and the 2D proxy can disagree with actual camera observability. The [fresh continuous protocol](fresh_protocol.md) was locked before its new missions. Its results must be interpreted separately because each arm remaps independently; the checkpoint reset cannot be counted as continuous autonomy.

**Separate conventional budget check.** The [predeclared read-only reserve replay](budget_replay_protocol.md) uses recorded map-planned route lengths and the public 18 m allowance, never target truth. With an explicitly assumed 4.9 m approach/replanning reserve, it admits nearest's old, center and west stations but rejects its late east leg (`20.36 m` projected total); it admits the gain policy's direct east leg (`16.93 m`). [Raw reserve decisions](../../results/matched_visibility_20261027/budget_replay.json). On the actual nearest trace, stopping before east would have saved about 5.9 m of evaluator-measured travel but would not have found ID7 or completed inspection. This is a **decision replay**, not a scored physical mission, and the 5 m detection horizon behind the reserve is unvalidated. It shows that a simple budget gate can report insufficient resource earlier; it cannot substitute for viewpoint ordering or a full budgeted informative-path planner.
