# Matched handoff diagnostic — frozen before execution

**Question.** On an identical acquired map and physical/estimated handoff, does the map-only visibility station selector lead to earlier *actual camera detection* and a complete physically valid inspection than the previous minimum-path-cost station selector? The opened wrong-asset case is primary; moved-target and target-absent cases are controls. All three cases are development data. This is an inspection-stage causal diagnostic, **not** an uninterrupted exploration-to-inspection result.

**Checkpoint construction.** For each case, use the saved acquisition/handoff of the earlier nearest-policy mission in `results/post_repair_connected_20261027`. Copy its exact acquired `inspection/map.txt`, public vision/commissioning files, controller config, scene, physical world and inspection evaluation. An evaluator-only script interpolates the physical robot pose at the recorded handoff timestamp and changes only the robot's initial `<pose>` in a copied SDF. That physical pose is supplied to Gazebo, **never to the policy**. Both arms receive the same saved AMCL initial estimate, variance, seed and map, with fresh wheel odometry after simulator restart. Rebase the policy clock (`handoff_time=0`, `max_sim_s=420`) to preserve the original 420 s inspection allowance; retain the original acquisition distance in the 35 m whole-task budget, the 18 m inspection path allowance, 36 captures and four stations. Copy all other inputs byte-for-byte. The reset and time rebasing are experimental mechanisms; both differ from a live handoff. Record all hashes, reset count and actual restart-state error. No target coordinate, visibility label, physical pose or successful viewpoint enters the policy.

**Only treatment.** Set `search_selector` absent (nearest) versus `search_selector: visibility_gain`. Same candidate lattice, map, camera, AMCL, marker/reader, planner, controller, approach logic, outcome contract and operator information. The new score predicts the **area of sampled free 2D map cells with unobstructed map-plane rays** from a fixed station, assuming a 360° scan over 0.6–5 m. It does *not* predict camera field of view, asset-facing side, marker pixels, elevated occlusion, reading quality or probability of ID7. The robot subsequently rotates through eight headings at each chosen station; actual images determine detection. Record predicted area per candidate, camera capture pose/heading, independent evaluator geometric visibility, decoded IDs, time/distance to first ID7, remaining 18 m/420 s at detection and confirmation, estimated/physical approach cost, final physical validity, contacts, false acceptance and reason.

**Predictions and alternatives.** On the frozen wrong-asset map, offline preview predicts east immediately after old rather than center/west. If the map cue improves actual camera coverage, ID7 should first decode earlier, leaving enough approach allowance, while ID8 stays rejected. If both policies decode ID7 at similar times but one fails, approach cost or localization is the likely difference. If predicted area is large yet the camera sees no asset, inspect heading/FOV, facing, range, elevated occlusion and resolution before changing the score. Moved-target checks regression and timing; absent-target checks honest bounded non-completion. Search-budget exhaustion does **not** certify physical absence. Successful inspection must have a valid **final claimed capture**, not merely some earlier valid capture.

**Execution and stop.** Freeze source/config/checkpoints before scoring. Run in this fixed sequential order: wrong/nearest, wrong/gain, moved/gain, moved/nearest, absent/nearest, absent/gain. One attempt per arm unless infrastructure prevents interpretation; retain and explain any rerun. Wall guard 800 s. Do not tune, add approach reserve, or change requirements during this comparison. If the wrong-asset pair diverges as predicted and controls do not expose a regression or false claim, freeze the selected conventional policy and only then construct a predeclared **new connected layout** for complete no-reset transfer missions. If no matched advantage, diagnose the earliest difference and do not select a fresh test to rescue the hypothesis. The six runs are three matched scenarios, not six independent environments.
