**Simulation, separately:** [IBM AIHWKit](https://github.com/IBM/aihwkit) is software for analog-device modeling, not access to a fabricated board. Its [hardware-aware training documentation](https://aihwkit.readthedocs.io/en/stable/hwa_training.html) describes PCM-like programming noise, drift, per-read effects and compensation; those parameters cannot be assumed to describe Mythic hardware. It is not installed or benchmarked here. Simulated delay or GPU execution does not measure analog board speed or energy.

**Next access step:** inspect public Mythic SDK/operator and update documentation and current distributor listings; if still unknown, prepare questions about availability, pricing, SDK terms, precision, supported dense/recurrent operators, reprogramming, latency and measurement boundary. Do not contact or buy without authorization. Once a digital learned policy exists, validate its operators and memory against ST's tools before considering a matched quantized comparison.

Inference-tile training uses ideal backward and update passes; this does not demonstrate physical on-chip online learning. The package is not installed or benchmarked here. Once a frozen digital policy exists, check its operator mapping and run paired digital, modeled analog, and modeled analog plus digital-adaptation evaluations, with a matched quantized digital reference.
